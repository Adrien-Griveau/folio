I am the founding designer of Linear, a specialized tool for product planning and development.

Originally from France and now based in Lisbon, Adrien has worked as a designer for companies such as Microsoft, Jour, and Hexa. With a deep passion for designing productivity software, he also enjoys working on visual identities and branding for ambitious companies.

Additionally, he is an angel investor, supporting early-stage startups.

In my free time, I pursue my creative interests in Music and photography.
